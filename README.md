# Covenant Floor · Cronos

The same floor as Covenant Floor, on Cronos. Lottery stays first. FELT is the chip. CRO pays gas.

## Tables

Lottery, pay desk, competition, dice, roulette, slots, plinko, keno, baccarat, hi-lo, mines, blackjack, poker, heads-up, shared table, wallet, stakes, tithe, pilgrimage, and the house bank.

## Rules

- Poker: 5,000 ante plus a 10,000 call returns 30,000 when the player wins with a pair the house qualifies under. Profit is 15,000.
- A net loss splits 15% burned, 5% buyback, 10% to stakers. 70% stays in the house.
- Pay desk: the brief locks first. Someone else submits. 48 hours to pay, or send it back with a reason. Silence pays the worker.
- House withdrawal is two of three owners, from the house bank only.

## Play

Connect a wallet on Cronos mainnet (chain 25). MetaMask or the Crypto.com Onchain Wallet. Gas is CRO.

FELT: `0x03b9d039e4a9de5c89c43b5917a39daacedea391`  
House: `0x828D84Ec3Ca9B307be03aD1658a6D98Bf2C4c269`

Dice, roulette, slots, plinko, the lottery, and the pay desk settle on the contract. Deposit FELT to the vault before a wager. The launched token has no free claim. The other tables use the same rules in the browser and do not move the Cronos vault.
